# Hi, I'm Jonathan

**Full-Stack Software Engineer**

I used to chase alpha, 5 years as a quantitative trader, writing Python to backtest 5-10 years of market data, developing intraday strategies for small caps and short-term swing strategies for mid-to-large caps. These days I chase clean architecture instead, but the instincts are the same: data-driven, detail-obsessed, and always hunting for the most efficient path from problem to solution. B.S. in Computer Science from Lehman College in New York, NY.

When I'm not at the computer, I'm probably out logging miles training for a half-marathon PR (marathon #1 in the works), somewhere new with a camera in hand, or still keeping half an eye on the market out of old habit.

### Featured Projects

**[Portfolio Website](https://jmontoya.dev)**
A minimalist portfolio with a built-in analytics dashboard, tracking sessions, clicks, hovers, and page views across dashboard, projects, and contact pages, visualized through charts.

**[Evoludent](https://evoludent.jmontoya.dev)**
A dental practice management platform that streamlines clinical workflows with Gemini-generated clinical notes, containerized with Docker and deployed via a GitHub Actions CI/CD pipeline to AWS.

**[MaxApp](https://maxapp.jmontoya.dev)**
An AI-powered job application tracker that helps you organize, prioritize, and stay on top of your job search and uses Google Gemini to review and tailor your resume to each job role and descriptions. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and MongoDB.
